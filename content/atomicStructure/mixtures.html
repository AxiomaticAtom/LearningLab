Pure Substances

A pure substance contains only one chemical.

Pure substances are either:

  • Elements
  • Compounds

Mixtures

A mixture is made of two or more substances physically combined but not chemically bonded.

You can have:

  • Mixtures of elements
  • Mixtures of compounds
  • Mixtures of elements and compounds

Examples of Mixtures

Mixtures vs Pure substances

All matter can be classified as either a pure substance or a mixture.

Homogeneous vs Heterogeneous Mixtures

A homogeneous mixture is one which is uniform. You cannot see or distinguish between its components.

A heterogeneous mixture is one which is not uniform. You can see or distinguish between its components.
