Key words

An atom is a very small particle. Most matter is made up of atoms.

A subatomic particle is an even smaller particle found inside an atom. The three subatomic particles are:

  • Protons
  • Neutrons
  • Electrons

The nucleus is a small, dense, positively charged object in the center of an atom made of protons and neutrons.

The space around the nucleus where electrons are usually found is called the electron cloud.
