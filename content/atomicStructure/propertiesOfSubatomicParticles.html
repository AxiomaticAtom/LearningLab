Properties of Subatomic Particles

Proton Neutron Electron
Charge +1 0 -1
Mass (u) 1 1 negligible (11840)
Location Inside nucleus Inside nucleus Outside nucleus (in the electron cloud)

*The mass of subatomic particles is measured using unified atomic mass units (u).

*The word negligible means "too small to have a significant effect, so it can be ignored during calculations".

Key words

Definition: A proton is a subatomic particle found inside the nucleus of an atom. Protons have a +1 charge and a mass of 1 u.

Definition: A neutron is a subatomic particle found inside the nucleus of an atom. Neutrons have no charge and a mass of 1 u.

Definition: An electron is a subatomic particle found outside the nucleus of an atom (in the electron cloud). Electrons have a charge of -1 and negligible mass.

Check your understanding

1. List the three types of subatomic particles.
  • Protons
  • Neutrons
  • Electrons
2. Which of the subatomic particles has a charge of a) +1, b) -1 and c) 0?
  • The proton has a charge of +1
  • The electron has a charge of -1
  • The neutron has a charge of 0
3. What is the mass of each type of subatomic particle?
  • Protons have a mass of 1 u (unified atomic mass units)
  • Neutrons have a mass of 1 u
  • Electrons have a mass of approximately 11840 u (i.e. negligible mass)
4. What does the word "negligible" mean, and which subatomic particle is said to have "negligible mass"?

Negligible means "too small to have a significant effect, so ir can be ignored during calculations". Electrons have negligible mass.
