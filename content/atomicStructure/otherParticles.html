Atoms

You already know that most of the matter around you is made of small particles called atoms, which are in turn composed of protons, neutrons and electrons.

Atoms

  • Atoms have equal numbers of protons and electrons. Each proton always has a +1 charge, and each electron always has a -1 charge.
  • These balance each other out perfectly.
  • As a result, atoms always have no net (overall) charge.

Atoms

Molecules

Atoms can stick together (bond) to form small particles called molecules.

Molecules usually form when atoms bond covalently (studied in detail later).

Molecules are also neutral overall.

Molecules

Ions

If an atom or molecule gains or loses electrons, there will no longer be a perfect balance between the numbers protons and electrons.

This means the particle will have a net charge.

Any particle with a net charge is referred to as an "ion".

Ions

A positive ion is referred to as a cation, and a negative ion is referred to as an anion.

Ions

Ions

Lattices

Ions can stick together into big repeating structures called crystal lattices. They are much bigger than molecules.

Crystal lattices are held together by ionic bonds (studied later).
