Electric Charge

There are two different types of Electric Charge an object can have.

  • Positive charge (+)
  • Negative charge (-)

Examples:

  • A proton has a +1 charge (a positive charge)
  • An electron has a -1 charge (a negative charge)
  • A neutron has a no charge (it is neutral)

Key Idea

  • Objects with the same sign of charge repel each other.
  • Objects with different signs of charges attract each other.

Make a Prediction

What do you think would happen if two protons were placed near each other?

Example

Protons have positive charge. If two protons are brought near each other, they will repel each.

Make a Prediction

What do you think would happen if two electrons were placed near each other?

Example

Electrons have negative charge. If two electrons are brought near each other, they will repel each.

Make a Prediction

What do you think would happen if a proton and electron were placed near each other?

Example

Opposite charges attract. A proton and an electron will attract each other.

Key words

A charge is a property that some objects have that causes them to attract or repel other charged objects.
